SkyRover™ Aircraft Platform

SkyRover is AlphaX Aerospace’s indigenous manned light aircraft platform, engineered for defence, pilot training, and reconnaissance missions.

Platform Overview

SkyRover is designed as a modular, mission-adaptable light aircraft platform addressing India’s growing requirements in defence training, surveillance, and pilot development. The platform prioritizes safety, endurance, pilot comfort, and operational flexibility.

SkyRover Variants

SkyRover Base

Optimized for pilot training and civilian operations with a strong focus on safety, reliability, and cost efficiency.

SkyRover Premium

Enhanced avionics, extended endurance, and superior comfort designed for advanced training and regional aviation roles.

SkyRover Tactical

Mission-ready configuration developed for defence, reconnaissance, and surveillance applications.

Operational Use Cases

  • Defence: Surveillance, reconnaissance, and tactical training support.
  • Pilot Training: Ab-initio and advanced pilot training platform.
  • Reconnaissance: Long-endurance observation and patrol missions.

Development Roadmap

AlphaX Aerospace is following a phased development approach for SkyRover, progressing from prototype validation to certification, followed by operational deployment across defence and training sectors.
