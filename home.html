The Problem

India relies on imported platforms for pilot training, surveillance, and light tactical aviation.

These platforms are expensive, rigid, and not designed for Indian operational realities.

Why AlphaX Exists

AlphaX Aerospace was founded to build indigenous, mission-adaptive aerospace platforms — engineered for scale, endurance, and sovereignty.

Our Aerospace Philosophy

AlphaX does not build aircraft as isolated products. We build systems designed to survive decades of operational evolution.

GlideCore™

Most aircraft treat pilot fatigue as an afterthought. AlphaX treats the human as part of the system.

GlideCore™ governs vibration, comfort, and control consistency at the airframe level.

FlightCore™

Aggressive automation fails in unpredictable missions. AlphaX prioritizes predictability over novelty.

FlightCore™ ensures stable, intuitive handling across training and operational environments.

PowerCore™

Aviation energy systems are not automotive systems. Safety margins, redundancy, and thermal discipline matter.

PowerCore™ is designed from aviation-first principles, not adapted from other industries.

AeroCore™

Speed is meaningless without structure. AeroCore™ governs airframe geometry, modularity, and long-term maintainability.

The Origin of SkyRover™

India lacked an indigenous, affordable, manned light aircraft platform adaptable across missions.

SkyRover™ was conceived to bridge this gap.

SkyRover Is a Platform

SkyRover™ was never designed as a single aircraft. It is a modular system capable of evolving without redesigning the airframe.

SkyRover Variants

Base — Pilot training & surveillance familiarization

Premium — Extended endurance & advanced avionics

Tactical — ISR-ready defence configuration
