ALPHAX™ Who We Are

AlphaX Aerospace Pvt Ltd is a deep-tech aerospace startup founded with the vision to make India self-reliant in advanced aviation technologies. Headquartered in India, AlphaX is pioneering indigenous design and manufacturing of next-generation aircraft. We are pioneering India’s first indigenous manned mini-aircraft – SkyRover. Built for defence, pilot training, and advanced air mobility, SkyRover blends innovation, comfort, and sustainability. Future of Flight. Made in India.
