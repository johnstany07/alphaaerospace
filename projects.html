Upcoming & Research Initiatives

In addition to its current aircraft platforms, AlphaX Aerospace is exploring future-oriented aerospace and defence concepts as part of its long-term research and innovation roadmap.

Anti-Landmine Detection Drone System (Concept)

AlphaX Aerospace is exploring a conceptual unmanned aerial system intended to assist in landmine detection and high-risk area reconnaissance.

The concept emphasizes non-invasive detection methods, modular payload integration, and remote operation to reduce human exposure in hazardous environments.

Defence-Oriented Unmanned Systems Research

AlphaX Aerospace is conducting early-stage research into unmanned aerial platforms for defence-oriented applications.

This initiative is conceptual in nature and aligned with applicable regulatory and legal frameworks.

SeaRover™ Maritime Surveillance Platform (Concept)

SeaRover™ is a conceptual maritime surveillance platform envisioned to support coastal monitoring and situational awareness operations.

The initiative explores coordination between aerial and maritime systems to enhance coverage in marine environments.

Nuclear-Powered Aircraft Research Initiative

AlphaX Aerospace maintains a long-term academic research interest in advanced propulsion concepts, including theoretical studies related to nuclear-powered aircraft.

This initiative is exploratory in nature and does not represent active development.

Exploratory Aerospace Research Programs

AlphaX Aerospace continuously evaluates emerging aerospace concepts through research, simulation, and feasibility studies to inform its long-term technology roadmap.
