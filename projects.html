AlphaX Aerospace Projects

AlphaX Aerospace is developing multiple indigenous aviation and aerospace platforms addressing training, defence, advanced mobility, and future flight technologies.

SkyRover™ Manned Light Aircraft

SkyRover™ is an indigenous manned light aircraft platform developed by AlphaX Aerospace for pilot training, reconnaissance, and advanced operational roles. The platform is designed with a strong focus on pilot comfort, handling stability, and mission adaptability.

SkyRover™ integrates AlphaX proprietary technologies such as GlideCore™ for vibration control, AeroCore™ for airframe and systems architecture, PowerCore™ for energy management readiness, and FlightCore™ for flight control and stability management.

eVTOL & Advanced Air Mobility Platform

AlphaX Aerospace is conceptualizing an electric vertical take-off and landing (eVTOL) platform focused on future urban and regional air mobility applications. The project emphasizes safety, modular propulsion architectures, and scalable design principles.

The platform is being designed to support future energy technologies and regulatory frameworks while maintaining a strong focus on pilot and passenger comfort.

Defence & Tactical Aviation Platforms

AlphaX Aerospace is developing conceptual and early-stage aviation platforms tailored for defence training, surveillance, and tactical support roles. These platforms emphasize mission flexibility, maintainability, and indigenous system integration.

The focus is on creating adaptable platforms that can evolve with changing operational requirements rather than single-role aircraft.

Upcoming & Research Initiatives

In addition to its current platforms, AlphaX Aerospace is exploring future-oriented aerospace and defence concepts as part of its long-term research and innovation roadmap.

Anti-Landmine Detection Drone System (Concept)

AlphaX Aerospace is exploring a conceptual unmanned aerial system designed to assist in landmine detection and area reconnaissance. The initiative focuses on improving safety and situational awareness in post-conflict and high-risk environments.

The concept emphasizes non-invasive detection methodologies, modular payload integration, and remote operation to reduce human exposure during survey operations.

Defence-Oriented Unmanned Systems Research

AlphaX Aerospace is conducting early-stage research into unmanned aerial platforms for defence-oriented applications. The focus is on platform stability, secure control architectures, and mission adaptability under controlled regulatory frameworks.

This research initiative is conceptual in nature and is intended to align with national defence requirements and applicable legal and regulatory guidelines.

SeaRover™ Maritime Surveillance Platform (Concept)

SeaRover™ is a conceptual maritime and coastal surveillance platform envisioned to support monitoring, reconnaissance, and situational awareness in marine environments.

The initiative explores multi-domain integration between aerial and maritime systems to enhance coverage and operational flexibility.

Nuclear-Powered Aircraft Research Initiative

AlphaX Aerospace maintains a long-term research interest in advanced propulsion concepts, including historical and theoretical studies on nuclear-powered aircraft.

This initiative is strictly exploratory and academic in nature, focusing on understanding feasibility challenges, safety constraints, and regulatory considerations rather than active development.

Exploratory & Future Aerospace Concepts

AlphaX Aerospace continuously evaluates emerging aerospace concepts through research, simulation, and feasibility analysis. These efforts inform the company’s long-term technology strategy and platform development roadmap.
