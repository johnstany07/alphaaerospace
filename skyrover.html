SkyRover™ Aircraft Platform

SkyRover™ is AlphaX Aerospace’s indigenous, mission-adaptive light aircraft platform engineered for defence, pilot training, and regional aviation applications.

Platform Overview

SkyRover is designed as a modular, scalable aircraft platform addressing India’s growing requirements in defence training, surveillance, and civilian pilot training. The platform prioritizes safety, endurance, pilot comfort, and operational flexibility.

Optimized for pilot training and civilian operations with a strong focus on safety, reliability, and cost effectiveness.

Enhanced avionics, extended endurance, and superior comfort designed for advanced training and regional aviation roles.

Mission-ready configuration developed for defence, reconnaissance, surveillance, and special operational requirements.

Operational Use Cases

  • Defence Training: Ab-initio and advanced pilot training
  • Surveillance & Reconnaissance: Long-endurance observation missions
  • Regional Aviation: Cost-efficient short-range operations

Development Roadmap

AlphaX Aerospace follows a phased development approach for SkyRover, progressing from prototype validation to certification, followed by operational deployment across defence and training sectors.
